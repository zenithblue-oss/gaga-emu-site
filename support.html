Support

Getting started

Why is my library empty?

GagaEmu ships with no games, ROMs or BIOS. Add homebrew or backups of cartridges and discs you own. GagaEmu does not sell, link to or help you obtain games.

How do I add games?
  • Open Add and choose from Files, or drop files onto the Add screen.
  • Copy files into the GagaEmu folder with the Files app (On My iPhone or On My iPad).
  • Turn on WiFi Transfer and open the address it shows in a browser on the same network. It is off by default and its traffic is unencrypted.
  • Connect your own WebDAV or RomM server from the Add screen.

Zip archives are unpacked on import. Multi-Disc Helper joins disc files into one playlist.

A system asks for a BIOS file. Where do I get it?

You supply BIOS files yourself, dumped from hardware you own. GagaEmu does not include or link to any. Settings shows the status of each core.

How do I apply a patch or ROM hack?

Use Patch a ROM on the Add screen. It applies an IPS, UPS or BPS patch to a copy, and leaves the original untouched.

Playing

Which controllers work?

Game controllers supported by iOS, including MFi, DualShock 4, DualSense, Xbox and Joy-Con controllers, and hardware keyboards. You can map every button, with turbo and macros.

Can I change the touch controls?

Yes. Move, resize and fade every button, pick a built-in skin such as Low Reach or Two Button, or import a .deltaskin layout.

Why does a game run slowly?

Every core is an interpreter with no JIT, so speed depends on your device and the game. 3D systems need a recent device, and some cores need an A14 chip or newer. GagaEmu is in early development and we make no promise that any particular title will run.

Where are my saves?

Save states and game saves are in the app's storage on your device, and in the Files app under On My iPhone or On My iPad. Manage them from the pause menu or Settings. With GagaEmu Pro you can sync them through iCloud.

GagaEmu Pro

What does Pro add?

Fast forward above 2x, more than 4 save states and 5 cheats per game, players 3 and 4, the full filter catalogue, iCloud sync, and extra app icons and accent colours. Every system and core is free without it.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings > GagaEmu Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription or ask for a refund?

Apple handles both. Manage subscriptions in the Settings app under your name > Subscriptions. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Nothing you made is removed if Pro ends.

About

Is GagaEmu open source?

Yes, under the GNU Affero General Public License 3.0. Bundled cores keep their own licences, listed in Settings > About > Licences. Source code.

Does GagaEmu collect data?

No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. See the privacy policy.

Contact

Report a bug or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Include your device, iOS version and the system you were playing. You can attach the session log from Settings.

Issues are public. Do not attach games, BIOS files or personal details.
