Frequently Asked Questions

How does GagaEmu run fast without JIT (Just-In-Time compilation)? ARCH

Apple strictly enforces W^X (Write XOR Execute) memory page protections on non-jailbroken devices, making standard JIT allocation illegal under App Store Rule 2.5.2. GagaEmu utilizes a heavily optimized direct-threaded opcode dispatcher with Clang tail-call elimination ([[clang::musttail]]), pre-decoded instruction tables, and hand-tuned ARM64 NEON SIMD intrinsics. This achieves steady 60 FPS in retro 8-bit, 16-bit, and 32-bit platforms with zero JIT required.

How do I transfer game backups to my iPhone or iPad? IMPORT

GagaEmu provides multiple ways to ingest files into your sandboxed library:

  • Local WiFi Transfer: Open the "Import" tab and tap "Start WiFi Server". GagaEmu will display an IP address (e.g. http://192.168.1.142:8080). Open that URL in your PC or Mac browser to drag-and-drop game files directly.
  • Apple Files App: Use the native iOS Files picker or iCloud Drive to import ROM files directly from your cloud storage.
  • Clipboard Importer: Copy a URL or data stream to your clipboard and tap "Paste from Clipboard" in the Ingestion Hub.

Which physical game controllers are supported? INPUT

GagaEmu is built directly on Apple's GameController.framework. It natively supports Sony PlayStation DualShock 4 and DualSense (PS5), Xbox Wireless Controllers, Nintendo Switch Joy-Cons (both paired dual and single horizontal modes), Switch Pro Controllers, Backbone One, Razer Kishi, and all MFi-certified gamepads. You can map up to 4 players simultaneously in the Controllers Modal.

How does the Multi-Core selection system work? CORES

GagaEmu allows you to swap emulation cores both globally and per-game. For example, for Game Boy Advance you can run mGBA for cycle accuracy or gpSP for maximum battery savings. For PS1, switch between Beetle PSX HW and PCSX-ReARMed. Long-press any title in your library and select "Core Selection" to assign an override.

Can I apply translation patches and ROM hacks? PATCH

Yes! GagaEmu features an integrated in-memory ROM patcher supporting IPS, BPS, and UPS patch formats. In the Ingestion Hub, tap "ROM Patcher", select your base legally dumped ROM, select your patch file, and GagaEmu will generate and verify the patched title with automated CRC32/MD5 checksum checking.

Where are my save files located? Can I sync them across devices? STORAGE

Your save states and battery save RAM (.srm / .sav) are saved directly in the app's sandboxed Documents container accessible through the iOS Files app under "On My iPhone / GagaEmu". If enabled in Settings, saves are synchronized end-to-end through your private iCloud Drive container to all your iPads and iPhones.
