A quiet shelf for your games.

GagaEmu plays homebrew and backups of the cartridges and discs you own, on iPhone and iPad, in portrait or landscape. Touch controls or a game controller. Every system is free to play, with no ads and no sign-in.

Coming soon to the App Store Source code on GitHub

No games included. GagaEmu comes with no games, ROMs or BIOS, and it does not sell, link to or help you get them. You add your own legally obtained files.

What it does

Your library, your controls.

Games sit on shelves by system. Continue keeps the last game one tap away. Play with touch buttons you can move and resize, or pair a controller.

Adding files

Bring your own games.

  • Choose from Files, or drop files onto the Add screen.
  • WiFi Transfer sends files from a browser on your network. It is off by default, and its traffic is unencrypted.
  • Zip archives are unpacked on import.
  • Multi-Disc Helper joins disc files into one playlist.
  • Patch a ROM applies an IPS, UPS or BPS patch to a copy. The original is left untouched.

iPhone and iPad

Room to play on iPad.

The same shelf, controls and saves on the big screen, upright or sideways. Save states, fast forward and rewind (where the system supports it) live in the pause menu.

Questions

FAQ

Does GagaEmu include any games or BIOS?

No. GagaEmu ships with no games, ROMs or BIOS. It does not sell, link to or help you obtain them. Use homebrew, or backups of cartridges and discs you own.

Does it use JIT?

No. Every core is built as an interpreter, with no JIT, dynamic recompiler or writable executable memory. You do not need to enable anything special on your device.

Which controllers work?

Game controllers supported by iOS, including MFi, DualShock 4, DualSense, Xbox and Joy-Con controllers, plus hardware keyboards.

Is it open source?

Yes. GagaEmu is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License 3.0. Bundled cores keep their own licences, listed in Settings > About > Licences. Source code.

Does it collect my data?

No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. Read the privacy policy for the optional features that talk to a server.

When can I get it?

GagaEmu is coming soon to the App Store. It is in early development, and we make no promise that any particular title will run.
