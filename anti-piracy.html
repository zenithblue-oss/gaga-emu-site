Anti-Piracy Policy & Legal Disclaimer

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 • Document Version 1.0.0

Zero-Tolerance Anti-Piracy Statement

GagaEmu is an independent, open-source emulation software framework developed solely for preservation, educational research, and software archival purposes. GagaEmu does NOT include, distribute, host, or link to any copyrighted game ROMs, ISOs, disc images, proprietary BIOS firmware, or licensed game assets.

1. Legal Basis of Video Game Emulation

Emulation software is legal under United States and international law, as established by landmark judicial precedents including Sony Computer Entertainment, Inc. v. Connectix Corp. (203 F.3d 596) and Sony Computer Entertainment America v. Bleem (214 F.3d 1022). Emulators function as hardware virtual machines developed via independent reverse engineering and clean-room implementation.

However, video game ROM files, disc images, and proprietary BIOS firmware files are protected by copyright laws. Emulators themselves are neutral technology platforms, akin to video cassette recorders or web browsers.

2. User Responsibilities & Fair Use Dumps

Users of GagaEmu must adhere strictly to the following rules:

  • You may only use GagaEmu with software you have legally acquired and physically dumped or ripped from original cartridges or discs that you personally own.
  • Downloading copyrighted commercial games or system BIOS files from third-party websites or peer-to-peer networks is copyright infringement in most jurisdictions and is strictly condemned by the GagaEmu development team.
  • Homebrew software, public domain games, and independently created titles operating under creative commons or permissive licenses are fully endorsed and welcomed.

3. Prohibition of Commercial Distribution & Bundling

No entity is permitted to bundle GagaEmu with unauthorized copyrighted ROMs or proprietary system firmware. Any commercial redistribution that includes copyrighted media without the explicit authorization of the copyright holders constitutes trademark and copyright infringement.

4. App Store Guidelines Adherence

GagaEmu complies with Apple's App Store Review Guideline 4.7 ("Mini apps, mini games, streaming games, chatbots, plug-ins, and game emulators"):

  • Guideline 4.7.1: Software offered in the app complies with all applicable laws and regulations.
  • Guideline 4.7.2: GagaEmu includes mechanisms to ensure users cannot access or download unlicensed games through in-app links or directories.
  • Guideline 2.5.2 & W^X: Operates entirely through deterministic interpreters and pre-decoded IR opcode blocks without runtime JIT compilation or forbidden memory remapping.
