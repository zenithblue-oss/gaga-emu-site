Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

Privacy Summary: We Do Not Collect Your Personal Data

GagaEmu was engineered from day one to respect your digital privacy. We do not track you, we do not collect personal identifiers, we do not embed third-party advertising SDKs, and we do not sell or monetize user data. All your game files, save states, and playtime statistics remain exclusively on your device or in your private Apple iCloud container.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

In accordance with App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1 (Data Collection and Storage):

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No Device Tracking or IDFA: GagaEmu does not access the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), Vendor Identifier (IDFV), or track users across external apps and websites.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: There is no Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or third-party behavioral tracker integrated into GagaEmu.
  • No Game Library Telemetry: We have no access to the names, quantities, or checksums of files you import into your library.

2. Local Storage & User Data Sandboxing

All data generated during your gameplay experience is stored exclusively inside your iOS app sandboxed container:

  • Save States and Battery Saves: Stored locally in the application's private sandboxed Documents directory.
  • iCloud Drive Synchronization: If you explicitly enable iCloud Sync, save files and configuration presets are synced through Apple's end-to-end encrypted iCloud ubiquity container directly between your personal Apple devices. The developers have no access to your iCloud account or its data.
  • Adventurer Profile & Statistics: Playtime counters, favorite cores, and custom avatars are stored locally via iOS UserDefaults and local CoreData/JSON files.

3. Local Area Network (WiFi Transfer Server)

GagaEmu contains an optional local HTTP file transfer server allowing users to drag and drop game backups and patches from desktop browsers:

  • The WiFi server runs strictly on your local subnet (LAN) on port 8080.
  • It makes zero outbound connections to remote external servers.
  • The server can only be started or stopped manually by you in the app's Ingestion Hub.

4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

Optional tips and premium unlocks (e.g. unlimited save states, premium shaders) are processed directly through Apple's native StoreKit 2 framework. Apple manages all financial transactions and payment data. We do not store or process your credit card numbers, billing information, or Apple ID credentials.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

GagaEmu does not target children under 13, nor does it knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application collects zero user data, it is fully compliant with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.

6. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain full rights over your data. Because GagaEmu does not collect, process, or transmit your personal data to any external server, you retain total ownership. Deleting the application or clearing the in-app cache instantly deletes all local data from your hardware.
