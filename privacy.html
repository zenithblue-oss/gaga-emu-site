Privacy Policy

In short: GagaEmu has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We, the developers, receive no data about you. Your games and saves stay on your device unless you share a file or turn on one of the optional features below.

What stays on your device

Your games, BIOS files, save files, save states, settings, skins and cheats are stored in the app's own storage on your device. You can see game files in the Files app under On My iPhone or On My iPad. Deleting the app deletes this data.

GagaEmu contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

Optional features that use the network

Each of these is off until you turn it on or sign in.

  • Cover Art (Settings > Library, off by default). When on, GagaEmu looks up box art for your games at thumbnails.libretro.com, a third-party service. The requests contain the matched game titles and the system name. They use HTTPS and no cookies. That service's own server logs are outside our control.
  • iCloud sync (GagaEmu Pro). When on, your saves sync between your devices through your own iCloud account, in the app's private iCloud container. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. We cannot see your iCloud data.
  • RetroAchievements. If you sign in, your username and password go over HTTPS to retroachievements.org. The password is not stored. The sign-in token is kept in this device's Keychain. While you are signed in, the game you play is identified to RetroAchievements so it can award achievements. RetroAchievements has its own privacy policy.
  • WebDAV and RomM servers. If you add your own server, GagaEmu connects only to the address you enter. Your sign-in for it stays in this device's Keychain.
  • WiFi Transfer (off by default). Runs a small file server on your local network so you can send files from a browser. It does not connect to the internet. Its traffic is unencrypted, so use it only on a network you trust.

Purchases

GagaEmu Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your payment details or Apple Account credentials. The app checks your purchase with Apple to unlock Pro.

Session logs

Settings has a button to share a session log for troubleshooting. Nothing is sent unless you tap it and choose where to share it.

Children

GagaEmu collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so do not include personal details.
